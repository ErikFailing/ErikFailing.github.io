{{- /* A project's write-ups: the published posts in the blog series named by the project's `writeups` front matter (the series' exact name, e.g. writeups = 'Fuzzing GameNetworkingSockets'), oldest first, which is reading order. Called with the project page; returns a slice of pages, empty when the project names no series or the series has no published posts yet (drafts don't count, so a card never links to a post that isn't live).
